# Give Thanks to the Lord — “Thankful for Who God Is” #

**Memory Verse:** “Enter his gates with thanksgiving and his courts with praise; give thanks to him and praise his name.” — Psalm 100:4 (NIV)

### Theme Specific

#### Thankful Leaves

Kick off the Thankful Wall for November. Each child decorates a paper leaf with “God is good” and one word describing God (kind, strong, loving, faithful). Add the leaves to the wall — we’ll add more every week until Thanksgiving.

### Scripture — Psalm 100 (NIV)

¹ Shout for joy to the Lord, all the earth. \
² Worship the Lord with gladness; \
come before him with joyful songs. \
³ Know that the Lord is God. \
It is he who made us, and we are his; \
we are his people, the sheep of his pasture.

⁴ Enter his gates with thanksgiving \
and his courts with praise; \
give thanks to him and praise his name. \
⁵ For the Lord is good and his love endures forever; \
his faithfulness continues through all generations.

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “The Gift and the Giver”

*You’ll need: a wrapped gift box with something fun inside (a small toy or candy), and a gift tag that says “From: Someone Who Loves You.”*

Hold up the gift and ask: “What’s the first thing you want to do when someone hands you a present?” (Open it!) Unwrap it and get excited about what’s inside.

Then hold up the tag. Say: “But who gave it? Sometimes we get so excited about the GIFT that we forget about the GIVER.”

Say: “God gives us SO many good things — food, families, friends, sunshine. But Psalm 100 says to thank God for who HE is. He made us. He is good. His love never ends. Even if the box were empty, the Giver would still be worth thanking.”

End: “This month we’re learning to be thankful. And we start with the Giver — God Himself.”

### Discussion Questions

- A psalm is a song. Who sang Psalm 100, and where were they going? (God’s people, walking into the temple.)

- Psalm 100 says we are “the sheep of his pasture.” What does it mean to belong to God like sheep belong to a shepherd?

- The psalm says to worship with gladness. What does a glad heart look like?

- What three things does verse 5 say about God? (He is good, His love lasts forever, He is faithful.)

- Name something about God — not a thing He gave you — that you are thankful for.

- Is God still good on a day when nothing goes your way?

### Activity (8 min) — Thank-You Gates

Two leaders face each other and make a “gate” with their arms.

Each child must name one thing they are thankful for to pass through the gate. Go around a few times — no repeats allowed!

On the last round, they have to name something about who God IS (kind, strong, loving, never changes) instead of something He gave.

Explain: “Psalm 100 tells God’s people to come to Him the same way — ‘Enter his gates with thanksgiving.’ We come to God with thank-yous!”

### Craft (5 min) — “We Are His” Sheep

Give each child a paper sheep outline and a handful of cotton balls.

Kids glue cotton balls on the sheep and draw a face.

Write across the bottom: “We are his people, the sheep of his pasture.” (Psalm 100:3)

Talk while they work: a good shepherd knows every sheep by name, and God knows each of them.

### Game (5 min) — Find the Shepherd’s Voice

One child is the “sheep” and closes their eyes (or wears a blindfold) in the middle of the room.

A leader quietly points to one child to be the “shepherd.” Everyone else makes sheep noises while the shepherd calls, “Come here, little sheep!”

The sheep follows the shepherd’s voice until they find them. Swap and play again.

Reinforces: we belong to God, and we learn to know His voice.

### Takeaways

- We thank God for who He is, not only for what He gives.

- God made us, and we belong to Him like sheep belong to their shepherd.

- The Lord is good, His love lasts forever, and He is faithful to every generation.

### Put It in Practice

Say Psalm 100:4 at dinner this week, and have everyone share one thing they’re thankful for about who God is.
