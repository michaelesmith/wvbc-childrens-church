# Jesus is God’s Plan for Forgiveness — Peter Preached About Jesus #

**Memory Verse:** “Peter replied, ‘Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins. And you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit.’” — Acts 2:38 (NIV)

### Scripture — Acts 2 (NIV) — Key Passages

#### The Holy Spirit Comes at Pentecost (Acts 2:1–4)

¹ When the day of Pentecost came, they were all together in one place. ² Suddenly a sound like the blowing of a violent wind came from heaven and filled the whole house where they were sitting. ³ They saw what seemed to be tongues of fire that separated and came to rest on each of them. ⁴ All of them were filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in other tongues as the Spirit enabled them.

#### Peter Preaches About Jesus (Acts 2:22–24)

²² “Fellow Israelites, listen to this: Jesus of Nazareth was a man accredited by God to you by miracles, wonders and signs, which God did among you through him, as you yourselves know. ²³ This man was handed over to you by God’s deliberate plan and foreknowledge; and you, with the help of wicked men, put him to death by nailing him to the cross. ²⁴ But God raised him from the dead, freeing him from the agony of death, because it was impossible for death to keep its hold on him.”

#### The Call to Respond (Acts 2:36–41)

³⁶ “Therefore let all Israel be assured of this: God has made this Jesus, whom you crucified, both Lord and Messiah.”

³⁷ When the people heard this, they were cut to the heart and said to Peter and the other apostles, “Brothers, what shall we do?”

³⁸ Peter replied, “Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins. And you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit. ³⁹ The promise is for you and your children and for all who are far off—for all whom the Lord our God will call.”

⁴⁰ With many other words he warned them; and he pleaded with them, “Save yourselves from this corrupt generation.” ⁴¹ Those who accepted his message were baptized, and about three thousand were added to their number that day.

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “The Stain and the Bleach”

*You’ll need: a small piece of white cloth, a marker or grape juice (to make a stain), and a small amount of stain remover or bleach (adult only, kept out of reach).*

Hold up the clean cloth and say: “This is how we started. Before anyone ever sinned, people were close to God and everything was good.”

Make a stain on the cloth (a single dark mark). Say: “Every time we sin—when we lie, disobey, say mean things, hurt people—we add a stain. We can’t scrub it out no matter how hard we try.” Try to wipe it; it won’t come clean.

Hold up the stain remover. Say: “Only ONE thing can truly clean a sin stain. When Peter preached, he told the people that Jesus died and rose again so that our sins could be totally washed away. If we turn from our sin and trust Jesus, He forgives us completely.”

Carefully apply the remover away from kids and let them see the stain fade. Say: “That’s what Jesus does for our hearts. Peter called it ‘repent and be baptized in the name of Jesus for the forgiveness of your sins.’ God’s plan for forgiveness is Jesus—nothing else and nobody else.”

### Discussion Questions

- What amazing thing happened at Pentecost? Why did God send the Holy Spirit?

- What was the main message of Peter’s sermon? Who did he preach about?

- What does the word “repent” mean? (Turn away from sin and turn toward Jesus.)

- What two things did Peter tell the people to do? (Repent + be baptized.)

- How many people believed that day? What does that tell us about God’s plan?

- Why is Jesus the ONLY way to be forgiven?

- Have you ever asked Jesus to forgive your sins? Would you like to talk about that today?

### Activity (8 min) — “Peter’s Sermon” Retell

Give each child a simple script card summarizing one part of Peter’s sermon: (1) God sent the Spirit, (2) Jesus did miracles, (3) Jesus died on the cross, (4) God raised Him, (5) Jesus is Lord and Messiah, (6) Repent and be baptized, (7) God’s promise is for everyone.

Kids stand in order and take turns “preaching” their part loudly and clearly, like Peter did that day.

At the end, everyone says together: “About 3,000 were added to their number that day!”

### Craft (5 min) — “Forgiven” Heart Card

Give each child two heart shapes: one has a dark stain drawn on it (“my sin”); the other is clean and has “FORGIVEN” written across it.

Staple them together so the clean heart covers the stained one like a flap.

On the back, write Acts 2:38 (short version): “Repent and be baptized… for the forgiveness of your sins.”

Kids can open and close it at home as a reminder of what Jesus has done.

### Game (5 min) — “Tell the Good News” Tag

Choose 2–3 “Peters.” Everyone else scatters around the play area.

When a “Peter” tags someone, that person has to say one thing about Jesus (He died, He rose, He forgives, He is Lord, etc.) before joining the “preaching” team.

Game ends when everyone is on the preaching team—just like the 3,000 people who responded to Peter’s sermon.

### Takeaways

- Forgiveness of sin is ONLY found in Jesus—He is God’s plan.

- The Holy Spirit helps us understand the gospel and share it with others.

- When we repent and trust Jesus, our sins are washed away and we become part of God’s family.

- The good news is for everyone—kids, grown-ups, neighbors, friends, and people far away.
