# Jesus Heals the Blind Man — “Jesus Helps Those in Need” #

**Memory Verse:** “‘Go,’ said Jesus, ‘your faith has healed you.’ Immediately he received his sight and followed Jesus along the road.” — Mark 10:52 (NIV)

### Scripture — Mark 10:46–52 (NIV)

⁴⁶ Then they came to Jericho. As Jesus and his disciples, together with a large crowd, were leaving the city, a blind man, Bartimaeus (which means “son of Timaeus”), was sitting by the roadside begging.

⁴⁷ When he heard that it was Jesus of Nazareth, he began to shout, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!” ⁴⁸ Many rebuked him and told him to be quiet, but he shouted all the more, “Son of David, have mercy on me!”

⁴⁹ Jesus stopped and said, “Call him.” So they called to the blind man, “Cheer up! On your feet! He’s calling you.” ⁵⁰ Throwing his cloak aside, he jumped to his feet and came to Jesus.

⁵¹ “What do you want me to do for you?” Jesus asked him. The blind man said, “Rabbi, I want to see.”

⁵² “Go,” said Jesus, “your faith has healed you.” Immediately he received his sight and followed Jesus along the road.

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “Blindfolded and Calling Out”

*You’ll need: a blindfold, and a small treat placed somewhere in the room.*

Blindfold one volunteer. Spin them gently, then say, “There’s a treat waiting for you — but you have to find it without seeing.” Let them try for a moment, bumping around.

Then say: “If you called out for help, would anyone answer?” Have the kids call directions. The blindfolded child follows the voices and finds the treat.

Take the blindfold off. Say: “Bartimaeus couldn’t see. He was ignored by the crowd. But when he heard Jesus was near, he called out — loud — and Jesus stopped for him. Jesus always stops for the ones who cry out to Him, even when others tell them to be quiet.”

### Discussion Questions

- What was Bartimaeus’s problem? Where was he sitting?

- What did he call Jesus? (“Son of David” — a name for the promised Messiah.)

- Why did people tell him to be quiet?

- Did that stop him? What did he do?

- What question did Jesus ask him? What was his answer?

- What happened when he was healed? (He followed Jesus.)

- What can we call out to Jesus for today?

### Activity (8 min) — Trust Walk

Pair kids up. One is blindfolded, the other is the guide.

The guide leads their partner around a simple obstacle course using only their voice — no touching.

Switch roles halfway through.

Talk after: “It takes trust to follow someone you can’t see. Bartimaeus trusted Jesus’ voice. We can trust Him too.”

### Craft (5 min) — “Now I See” Glasses

Give each child a pair of paper glasses cut from cardstock.

On one lens they write “JESUS.” On the other they write “FAITH.”

Decorate the frames. Attach a paper strap or a craft stick so they can wear them.

Reminder: real seeing happens when we look to Jesus in faith.

### Game (5 min) — “Call the Master”

One child stands at one end of the room with eyes closed — they are “Bartimaeus.” Another child is “Jesus” on the opposite side.

Bartimaeus calls out, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!”

Jesus responds, “Come!” Bartimaeus walks (eyes closed) toward the voice until he reaches Jesus.

Rotate so everyone plays both roles.

### Takeaways

- Jesus sees and hears people others ignore.

- Never stop calling on Jesus — even when others tell you to be quiet.

- When Jesus meets our need, our response is to follow Him.
