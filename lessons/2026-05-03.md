# Jesus is God’s Perfect Son — The Baptism of Jesus #

**Memory Verse:** “And a voice from heaven said, ‘This is my Son, whom I love; with him I am well pleased.’” — Matthew 3:17 (NIV)

### Scripture — Matthew 3:13–17; Mark 1:1–11; John 1:29–34 (NIV)

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “Clean White Cloth, Dirty World”

*You’ll need: a perfectly clean, bright white cloth or T-shirt, a bowl of muddy water, and a second dirty rag.*

Hold up the dirty rag and say: “This rag is like us—sometimes we do wrong things, think mean thoughts, or disobey. The Bible calls this sin. No matter how hard we try, we can’t clean ourselves up perfectly.” Dunk the dirty rag in muddy water; it comes out even dirtier.

Hold up the clean white cloth and say: “Jesus is like THIS. He is perfectly clean. He NEVER sinned, not one time. He didn’t need to be baptized for His own sin because He had none.”

Ask: “Then why did Jesus get baptized?” Let the kids think. Say: “Jesus was baptized to stand with us. He was showing that He would take our place—that the perfect Son of God would one day take our dirty sin on Himself so we could be made clean.”

End: “When God the Father looked down at that moment and said, ‘This is my Son, whom I love,’ He was pointing to the perfect, spotless Savior. And the best news? Jesus offers to trade His clean for our dirty when we trust Him.”

### Discussion Questions

- Who baptized Jesus? Where did it happen?

- Why did John feel like HE should be baptized by Jesus instead?

- What three amazing things happened when Jesus came up out of the water? (Heaven opened, Spirit like a dove, Father’s voice.)

- What did God the Father say about Jesus? What does that tell us?

- Jesus had no sin—so why do you think He chose to be baptized?

- Have you ever seen a baptism? What does baptism show about a person’s faith?

### Activity (8 min) — “The Baptism Scene” Tableau

Assign roles: Jesus, John the Baptist, the crowd, the dove (Spirit), the voice of the Father.

Walk through the scene slowly: Jesus steps forward, John is hesitant, Jesus goes down into the “Jordan” (blue cloth on the floor), comes up, the “dove” lands on His shoulder, and the “voice” speaks Matthew 3:17.

Freeze the scene like a living picture and have everyone say the memory verse together.

Talk briefly about each person’s part in showing that Jesus is God’s perfect Son.

### Craft (5 min) — Dove Memory Verse Mobile

Give each child a dove shape cut from white paper and a cloud shape cut from blue paper.

On the dove, they write: “This is my Son, whom I love” (Matthew 3:17).

On the cloud, they draw the Jordan River and a little figure of Jesus.

Tie a string so the dove hangs above the cloud—reminding them that the Father in heaven is well pleased with His Son.

### Game (5 min) — “Three Signs” Relay

Set up three stations across the room: (1) a paper “open sky,” (2) a paper “dove,” (3) a card with “The Father’s Voice.”

Divide into two teams. When called, a player runs to each station, names what happened at Jesus’ baptism, then runs back and tags the next player.

First team to finish wins. Reinforces the three signs that showed Jesus is God’s perfect Son.

### Takeaways

- Jesus is perfect—He never sinned, not even once.

- At His baptism, the Father, the Son, and the Spirit all showed up together—proving Jesus is God’s beloved, perfect Son.

- Jesus was baptized to stand with us and start His mission to rescue us from sin.
