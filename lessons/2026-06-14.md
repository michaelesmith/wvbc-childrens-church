# Jesus is the Son of God Who Calls Us to Follow #

**Memory Verse:** “Then Nathanael declared, ‘Rabbi, you are the Son of God; you are the king of Israel.’” — John 1:49 (NIV)

### Scripture — John 1:35–51 (NIV)

³⁵ The next day John was there again with two of his disciples. ³⁶ When he saw Jesus passing by, he said, “Look, the Lamb of God!”

³⁷ When the two disciples heard him say this, they followed Jesus. ³⁸ Turning around, Jesus saw them following and asked, “What do you want?” They said, “Rabbi” (which means “Teacher”), “where are you staying?”

³⁹ “Come,” he replied, “and you will see.” So they went and saw where he was staying, and they spent that day with him. It was about four in the afternoon.

⁴⁰ Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, was one of the two who heard what John had said and who had followed Jesus. ⁴¹ The first thing Andrew did was to find his brother Simon and tell him, “We have found the Messiah” (that is, the Christ). ⁴² And he brought him to Jesus.

Jesus looked at him and said, “You are Simon son of John. You will be called Cephas” (which, when translated, is Peter).

⁴³ The next day Jesus decided to leave for Galilee. Finding Philip, he said to him, “Follow me.”

⁴⁴ Philip, like Andrew and Peter, was from the town of Bethsaida. ⁴⁵ Philip found Nathanael and told him, “We have found the one Moses wrote about in the Law, and about whom the prophets also wrote—Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.”

⁴⁶ “Nazareth! Can anything good come from there?” Nathanael asked. “Come and see,” said Philip.

⁴⁷ When Jesus saw Nathanael approaching, he said of him, “Here truly is an Israelite in whom there is no deceit.”

⁴⁸ “How do you know me?” Nathanael asked. Jesus answered, “I saw you while you were still under the fig tree before Philip called you.”

⁴⁹ Then Nathanael declared, “Rabbi, you are the Son of God; you are the king of Israel.”

⁵⁰ Jesus said, “You believe because I told you I saw you under the fig tree. You will see greater things than that.” ⁵¹ He then added, “Very truly I tell you, you will see ‘heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending on’ the Son of Man.”

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “Come and See”

*You’ll need: a shoebox with a peephole cut in one end (about the size of a quarter), and a small surprise inside (a picture of Jesus, a cross, a tiny figurine, or a hand-written note that says “JESUS IS THE SON OF GOD”).*

Hold up the closed shoebox. Say: “There’s something amazing inside this box. From where you’re sitting, can you tell what it is?” Let kids guess wildly from a distance.

Say: “You can’t really know what’s inside until you do one thing: COME closer, and SEE for yourself.” Invite kids one at a time to peek through the peephole. Let them whisper what they saw without spoiling it for the others.

When everyone has peeked, say: “Today’s story is about people who heard about Jesus from far away—and they had to decide whether to come closer and see for themselves. When Nathanael said, ‘Can anything good come from Nazareth?’ his friend Philip didn’t argue. He just said, ‘Come and see.’ And when Nathanael came and met Jesus, he KNEW. He said, ‘You are the Son of God!’”

End: “Jesus invites YOU to come and see, too. Not from far away. Not just hearing about Him. He says, ‘Come, follow Me, and you will see who I really am.’”

### Discussion Questions

- How many disciples does Jesus call in this passage? Can you name them? (Andrew, Peter, Philip, Nathanael—plus the unnamed disciple, probably John.)

- What different ways does Jesus call people in this story? (Some hear from John; some follow on their own; some Jesus calls directly; some are brought by a friend.)

- Why was Nathanael surprised when he heard about Jesus of Nazareth?

- What did Andrew do right after meeting Jesus? (He went and got his brother.) Who could YOU bring to Jesus?

- What does it mean to “follow” Jesus today? (Listen to Him, obey Him, spend time with Him, go where He leads.)

- If a friend asked you, “Why do you follow Jesus?” what would you say?

- Nathanael said, “Rabbi, you are the Son of God.” What helped him believe?

### Activity (8 min) — “Bring a Friend” Chain Reaction

Pick one child to play Jesus, who stands at one end of the room.

Jesus calls one child to follow Him (Andrew). That child runs to Jesus, then goes back and brings a friend (Peter) to Jesus.

Jesus then calls another child directly (Philip). Philip goes and brings a friend (Nathanael) to Jesus.

Continue until everyone is gathered around Jesus.

Talk briefly: “Look how fast the good news of Jesus spreads when each person tells just ONE other person. That’s exactly how Jesus’ church grew—friend by friend, family member by family member.”

### Craft (5 min) — “You’re Invited” Card

Give each child a folded piece of cardstock to make an invitation card.

Front: “YOU’RE INVITED” with decorations (stickers, drawings).

Inside top: “To follow Jesus.”

Inside bottom: write the memory verse and Philip’s words, “Come and see” (John 1:46).

Encourage them: “Pick one friend or family member this week and give them this invitation. Be like Andrew. Be like Philip.”

### Game (5 min) — “Jesus Says” (Simon Says Twist)

Play like Simon Says, but the leader is Jesus.

Kids only do an action if the leader says, “Jesus says…”

If the leader just says “Hop on one foot” (no “Jesus says”), and a child does it, they sit down.

Last child standing wins—and gets to be the next “Jesus” caller.

Reinforces: we follow Jesus when He calls; we listen for HIS voice, not every voice.

### Takeaways

- Jesus personally invites each of us to follow Him.

- We “come and see” Jesus by reading the Bible, praying, and walking with other believers.

- After we meet Jesus, we share Him with others—just like Andrew brought Peter and Philip brought Nathanael.

- Nathanael’s declaration is true for us too: Jesus is the Son of God; He is the King.
