# Young Jesus in the Temple — “Jesus in His Father’s House” #

**Memory Verse:** “‘Why were you searching for me?’ he asked. ‘Didn’t you know I had to be in my Father’s house?’” — Luke 2:49 (NIV)

### Scripture — Luke 2:41–52 (NIV)

⁴¹ Every year Jesus’ parents went to Jerusalem for the Festival of the Passover. ⁴² When he was twelve years old, they went up to the festival, according to the custom.

⁴³ After the festival was over, while his parents were returning home, the boy Jesus stayed behind in Jerusalem, but they were unaware of it. ⁴⁴ Thinking he was in their company, they traveled on for a day. Then they began looking for him among their relatives and friends. ⁴⁵ When they did not find him, they went back to Jerusalem to look for him.

⁴⁶ After three days they found him in the temple courts, sitting among the teachers, listening to them and asking them questions. ⁴⁷ Everyone who heard him was amazed at his understanding and his answers.

⁴⁸ When his parents saw him, they were astonished. His mother said to him, “Son, why have you treated us like this? Your father and I have been anxiously searching for you.”

⁴⁹ “Why were you searching for me?” he asked. “Didn’t you know I had to be in my Father’s house?” ⁵⁰ But they did not understand what he was saying to them.

⁵¹ Then he went down to Nazareth with them and was obedient to them. But his mother treasured all these things in her heart. ⁵² And Jesus grew in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and man.

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “Where Do You Find Him?”

*You’ll need: three or four different “home” pictures (or props) — a house, a school, a park, and a church. Also a small photo/figure of Jesus.*

Hide the Jesus figure in the “church” picture. Show each of the other locations first and ask: “Is Jesus here?” Kids will guess.

When you get to the church, reveal Jesus. Say: “Jesus was 12 years old — the same age as some of you! When His parents lost track of Him, they searched everywhere. Where did they finally find Him? In the temple — God’s house.”

Say: “Jesus wasn’t lost. He knew exactly where He wanted to be: with His Father. When we want to be near God, we go to His house too — to church, to the Bible, and to prayer.”

### Discussion Questions

- How old was Jesus in this story? (Twelve — same age as some of you!)

- What festival were Jesus’ family celebrating in Jerusalem?

- How long did it take Mary and Joseph to notice Jesus was missing?

- Where did they find Him? What was He doing?

- What did Jesus mean by “my Father’s house”?

- Where do WE go to be with our Heavenly Father today?

- Verse 52 says Jesus grew in wisdom — how can we grow in wisdom too?

### Activity (8 min) — “Listen and Ask” Circle

Sit in a circle like Jesus did with the teachers in the temple.

The leader poses simple Bible questions (“Who made the world?” “Who was in the belly of a big fish?” “What was Jesus’ mom’s name?”).

Kids listen, then answer OR ask their own Bible question.

Praise the good questions — that’s exactly what young Jesus did. Great learners ask great questions.

### Craft (5 min) — “My Father’s House” Fold-Up Church

Give each child cardstock cut into a simple church shape with a cross on top.

On the outside, they decorate with markers (windows, doors, a bell).

Fold it open. Inside, write: “I want to be in my Father’s house.” — Luke 2:49

Stand it up on the mantel or nightstand at home as a reminder.

### Game (5 min) — “Seek the Word” Bible Hide-and-Find

Hide a small Bible somewhere in the room.

Give the kids a single clue: “It’s in the Father’s house.”

First one to find it wins and gets to hide it for the next round.

Reinforces: Mary and Joseph searched three days to find Jesus — seeking God is worth it.

### Takeaways

- Even as a boy, Jesus loved being where God was.

- We meet with our Father through the Bible, prayer, and church.

- Jesus grew in wisdom — and we can too, by spending time with God.
