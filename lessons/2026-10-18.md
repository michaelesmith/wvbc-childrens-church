# Zacchaeus Meets Jesus — “Jesus Changes Hearts” #

**Memory Verse:** “‘For the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost.’” — Luke 19:10 (NIV)

### Scripture — Luke 19:1–10 (NIV)

¹ Jesus entered Jericho and was passing through. ² A man was there by the name of Zacchaeus; he was a chief tax collector and was wealthy. ³ He wanted to see who Jesus was, but because he was short he could not see over the crowd. ⁴ So he ran ahead and climbed a sycamore-fig tree to see him, since Jesus was coming that way.

⁵ When Jesus reached the spot, he looked up and said to him, “Zacchaeus, come down immediately. I must stay at your house today.” ⁶ So he came down at once and welcomed him gladly.

⁷ All the people saw this and began to mutter, “He has gone to be the guest of a sinner.”

⁸ But Zacchaeus stood up and said to the Lord, “Look, Lord! Here and now I give half of my possessions to the poor, and if I have cheated anybody out of anything, I will pay back four times the amount.”

⁹ Jesus said to him, “Today salvation has come to this house, because this man too is a son of Abraham. ¹⁰ For the Son of Man came to seek and to save the lost.”

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “Before and After Heart”

*You’ll need: two paper hearts. One is stained/marked with dark spots and dollar signs. The other is clean and colorful.*

Hold up the stained heart. Say: “This is Zacchaeus’s heart before he met Jesus. He was a tax collector who took extra money from people. He was rich but his heart was messy. People hated him. He probably didn’t like himself much either.”

Say: “Zacchaeus climbed a tree just to see Jesus. And what did Jesus do? He CALLED HIM BY NAME. ‘Zacchaeus, come down!’ He wanted to be with him.”

Hold up the clean heart. Say: “One meeting with Jesus, and Zacchaeus’s heart CHANGED. He gave half his stuff to the poor. He paid back FOUR TIMES what he’d cheated. Jesus didn’t just clean up his behavior — He cleaned up his HEART.”

End: “That’s what Jesus does. He doesn’t come to make bad people better. He comes to make dead hearts ALIVE.”

### Discussion Questions

- What was Zacchaeus’s job? Why did people not like him?

- Why did he climb the tree?

- What did Jesus say when He saw Zacchaeus up there?

- How did the crowd react?

- How did Zacchaeus change after meeting Jesus?

- What does it mean that Jesus came to “seek and to save the lost”?

- Can meeting Jesus change YOUR heart? How?

### Activity (8 min) — “Climb to See Jesus”

Set up a low bench, sturdy chair, or step in the room — that’s the “sycamore tree.”

One at a time, each child stands on the “tree”. The leader (playing Jesus) walks by, looks up, and says: “\[Their name\], come down! I want to spend time with you today.”

The child climbs down and gets a high-five or hug.

Talk after: “Jesus knows your name. He wants to spend time with YOU today, just like He did with Zacchaeus.”

### Craft (5 min) — Zacchaeus in a Tree Slider

Give each child a sycamore tree cut from green construction paper.

Cut a vertical slit in the trunk. Give them a Zacchaeus figure on a tab that can slide up and down through the slit.

On the back of the tree, write: “Jesus called MY name.”

They can slide Zacchaeus up the tree and back down to remember the story.

### Game (5 min) — “Come Down!” Freeze

One kid is “Jesus” and stands at one end of the room.

Everyone else — the “Zacchaeuses” — walks around “climbing trees” (making tree poses).

“Jesus” calls out a name. That kid has to freeze in a “I’m coming down!” pose.

Everyone gets called eventually. Reinforces: Jesus knows and calls each one of us by name.

### Takeaways

- Jesus notices people that everyone else ignores.

- Meeting Jesus changes us from the inside out.

- Jesus came to seek and save the lost — and that includes YOU.

- Real change shows up in how we treat others (like Zacchaeus paying back what he took).
