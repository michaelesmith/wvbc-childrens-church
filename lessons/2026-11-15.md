# Paul and Silas in Prison — “Thankful in Hard Times” #

**Memory Verse:** “Give thanks in all circumstances; for this is God’s will for you in Christ Jesus.” — 1 Thessalonians 5:18 (NIV)

### Theme Specific

#### Thankful Leaves

Each child adds a leaf to the Thankful Wall for something good God gave them during a hard time (along with their Broken Chain links).

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “Shine Under Pressure”

*You’ll need: a glow stick for each child (and one for the leader), and a way to dim the lights.*

Hold up an uncracked glow stick. Say: “This glow stick is fine, but it isn’t doing much.”

Bend it until it cracks. Say: “Sometimes life bends us — a hard test, a sick pet, a friend who moves away. It hurts.” Shake it and turn the lights down. It glows!

Say: “Paul and Silas were beaten and locked up in the dark. But instead of complaining, they prayed and sang to God — and their thankful hearts SHONE. The other prisoners were listening. The jailer and his whole family came to know Jesus that night!”

End: “When we thank God in hard times, His light shines through us — and other people notice.” Hand out the rest of the glow sticks for kids to crack.

### Discussion Questions

- Why were Paul and Silas thrown into prison? (They were telling people about Jesus.)

- What were they doing at midnight? Who was listening? (v. 25)

- What happened when the earthquake came? Why didn’t Paul and Silas run away?

- What question did the jailer ask, and what was the answer? (v. 30–31)

- Why could Paul and Silas sing in such a bad place?

- Who might be watching how you act on a hard day?

- Tell about a hard time when God still gave you something to be thankful for.

### Activity (8 min) — Midnight in the Prison

Act out the story. Assign roles: Paul, Silas, the jailer, the jailer’s family, and other prisoners (everyone else).

Turn the lights down low. Paul and Silas sit with their feet “in the stocks” (inside a hula hoop or taped square).

At “midnight,” Paul and Silas start singing a simple praise song, and the prisoners lean in to listen. Then everyone joins in quietly.

Leader shouts “Earthquake!” — everyone stomps and shakes, and the “chains” (paper or streamers) fall off.

The jailer rushes in, Paul calls out “We are all here!”, and the jailer asks, “What must I do to be saved?” Everyone answers together: “Believe in the Lord Jesus!” (v. 31)

### Craft (5 min) — Broken Chains

Give each child several paper strips to make a paper chain.

On the front of each link, kids write or draw a hard thing (a test, a sick pet, moving, being left out).

On the back, they write one way God helped or something good that came from it.

“Break” the chain at the end and add the links to the Thankful Wall — God can turn hard things into reasons to thank Him.

### Game (5 min) — Earthquake Freeze

Kids walk around the room while music plays. The leader calls out one of three commands:

- **“Midnight!”** — everyone sits down and sings one line of a praise song.
- **“Earthquake!”** — everyone shakes and wobbles.
- **“Jailer!”** — everyone freezes. The last one to freeze says the memory verse (with help if needed).

Play several rounds, mixing up the commands faster each time.

### Takeaways

- We can thank God even when things are hard, because He is still with us.

- Paul and Silas prayed and sang in a dark prison — and God was at work the whole time.

- Thankfulness in hard times shines, and other people notice.

- “Give thanks in all circumstances” — not just the good ones.

### Put It in Practice

Practice 1 Thessalonians 5:18. The next time something goes wrong this week, stop and focus on what you’re thankful for.
