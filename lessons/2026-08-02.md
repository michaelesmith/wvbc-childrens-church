# Jesus Loves the Children — “Jesus Loves Me” #

**Memory Verse:** “‘Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these.’” — Mark 10:14 (NIV)

### Scripture — Mark 10:13–16 (NIV)

¹³ People were bringing little children to Jesus for him to place his hands on them, but the disciples rebuked them.

¹⁴ When Jesus saw this, he was indignant. He said to them, “Let the little children come to me, and do not hinder them, for the kingdom of God belongs to such as these. ¹⁵ Truly I tell you, anyone who will not receive the kingdom of God like a little child will never enter it.”

¹⁶ And he took the children in his arms, placed his hands on them and blessed them.

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “Welcome Sign”

*You’ll need: two signs — one that says “DO NOT ENTER” and one that says “WELCOME.”*

Hold up the DO NOT ENTER sign first. “That’s what the disciples said when parents brought their little kids to Jesus. They said, ‘Not now, kids. Jesus is too busy.’”

Switch to the WELCOME sign. “But Jesus flipped the sign. He said, ‘Let the little children COME to me!’ Jesus never puts up a ‘do not enter’ sign for kids. He always has a WELCOME sign out for you.”

Tape the WELCOME sign at kid-height on the wall. Say: “That sign is always up in Jesus’ heart for YOU.”

### Discussion Questions

- Who was trying to stop the children from coming to Jesus?

- How did Jesus feel about that? (Indignant — upset!)

- What did Jesus DO with the children? (Took them in His arms, blessed them.)

- What does it mean that God’s kingdom belongs to “such as these”?

- What does it mean to receive God’s kingdom like a child?

- How do you know Jesus loves YOU personally?

### Activity (8 min) — Circle of Blessing

All the kids stand or sit in a circle.

Go around the circle. For each child, the leader (and other kids too, if they want) says one true, kind thing over them: “God made you special.” “Jesus loves you.” “You are brave.” “You are precious to God.”

The child being blessed just receives it. No performance needed.

Talk after: “That’s what Jesus did that day — He blessed every child.”

### Craft (5 min) — Handprint “Jesus Loves ME”

Trace each child’s hand on colored paper.

Inside the palm write in big letters: “JESUS LOVES ME.”

On each finger write a word describing them: kind, brave, funny, smart, loved.

Cut it out and tape to their bedroom door at home.

### Game (5 min) — “Run to Jesus” Musical Freeze

Place a paper cross in the center of the room.

Play music. Kids dance and move around.

When the music stops, everyone runs to the cross and sits around it — “in Jesus’ arms.”

No one is ever out. Everyone always gets to come to Jesus.

### Takeaways

- Jesus loves children personally — including YOU.

- Nothing can keep you from Jesus. He always has a “welcome” sign out.

- Coming to Jesus with childlike trust is the key to God’s kingdom.
