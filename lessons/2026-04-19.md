# Jesus is God’s Promised Son #

**Memory Verse:** “Simon Peter answered, ‘You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God.’” — Matthew 16:16 (NIV)

### Scripture — Matthew 16:13–20 (NIV)

¹³ When Jesus came to the region of Caesarea Philippi, he asked his disciples, “Who do people say the Son of Man is?”

¹⁴ They replied, “Some say John the Baptist; others say Elijah; and still others, Jeremiah or one of the prophets.”

¹⁵ “But what about you?” he asked. “Who do you say I am?”

¹⁶ Simon Peter answered, “You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God.”

¹⁷ Jesus replied, “Blessed are you, Simon son of Jonah, for this was not revealed to you by flesh and blood, but by my Father in heaven. ¹⁸ And I tell you that you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of Hades will not overcome it. ¹⁹ I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven; whatever you bind on earth will be bound in heaven, and whatever you loose on earth will be loosed in heaven.”

²⁰ Then he ordered his disciples not to tell anyone that he was the Messiah.

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “The Sealed Promise”

*You’ll need: a sealed envelope labeled “GOD’S PROMISE” containing a card that says “The Messiah is coming!”, and a second envelope or gift bag labeled “FULFILLED” containing a small picture of Jesus (or a card that says “JESUS”).*

Hold up the sealed “GOD’S PROMISE” envelope and say: “Long, long ago—hundreds and even thousands of years before Jesus was born—God made a promise to His people. He said He was going to send a special Rescuer called the Messiah. God spoke this promise through prophets like Isaiah, Micah, and Jeremiah. The people waited… and waited… and waited. Some of them tried to guess who it might be. Some thought a prophet like Elijah. Some thought a powerful king who would fight the Romans. Some even thought John the Baptist was the one.”

Ask the kids to guess what’s inside the envelope. Take a few answers. Then slowly open it and show the card: “The Messiah is coming!”

Bring out the second envelope labeled “FULFILLED” and say: “But God didn’t just make a promise and forget about it. God ALWAYS keeps His promises. When the time was just right, God sent the answer.” Open the envelope and reveal the picture of Jesus.

Say: “When Peter looked at Jesus and said, ‘You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God,’ he was saying, ‘YOU are the One God promised! You’re the answer we’ve been waiting for!’ Jesus is God’s promise kept.”

### Discussion Questions

- Who did people in Jesus’ day think He was? Who do people today say Jesus is?

- What does the word “Messiah” mean? (Answer: God’s chosen and promised Rescuer.)

- How did Peter know the right answer? (God revealed it to him.)

- Who do YOU say Jesus is? Why does your answer matter?

- Can you think of any Old Testament promises about the Messiah? (Isaiah 9:6; Micah 5:2)

### Activity (8 min) — “Who Do You Say I Am?” Role Play

Have kids sit in a circle. One child plays Jesus and walks around asking, “Who do people say I am?” Others call out the answers the crowds gave (John the Baptist, Elijah, a prophet).

Then “Jesus” stops in front of one child and asks, “But who do YOU say I am?” That child answers with the memory verse: “You are the Messiah, the Son of the living God.”

Rotate roles so everyone has a turn to answer.

### Craft (5 min) — “I Believe” Foldable Card

Give each child a piece of cardstock folded in half.

On the front, have them write “Who is Jesus?” and decorate with a crown or cross.

Inside, have them write Matthew 16:16 and illustrate it.

Kids take these home as a reminder of their own confession.

### Game (5 min) — “Messiah Promise Match”

Prepare cards with Old Testament promises on one set (e.g., “Born in Bethlehem,” “Called Immanuel,” “From the line of David,” “Pierced for our sins”).

On the other set, write how Jesus fulfilled each promise.

Kids race to match the promise to the fulfillment, showing Jesus is the promised Son God spoke about long before He came.

### Takeaways

- Jesus isn’t just a good teacher or a prophet—He is the Messiah, the promised Son of the living God.

- God had promised this Rescuer for thousands of years, and Jesus is the fulfillment of that promise.

- Knowing who Jesus really is changes everything about how we live and trust Him.
