# Jesus Heals the Ten Lepers — “Saying Thank You” #

**Memory Verse:** “One of them, when he saw he was healed, came back, praising God in a loud voice.” — Luke 17:15 (NIV)

### Scripture — Luke 17:11–19 (NIV)

¹¹ Now on his way to Jerusalem, Jesus traveled along the border between Samaria and Galilee. ¹² As he was going into a village, ten men who had leprosy met him. They stood at a distance ¹³ and called out in a loud voice, “Jesus, Master, have pity on us!”

¹⁴ When he saw them, he said, “Go, show yourselves to the priests.” And as they went, they were cleansed.

¹⁵ One of them, when he saw he was healed, came back, praising God in a loud voice. ¹⁶ He threw himself at Jesus’ feet and thanked him—and he was a Samaritan.

¹⁷ Jesus asked, “Were not all ten cleansed? Where are the other nine? ¹⁸ Has no one returned to give praise to God except this foreigner?”

¹⁹ Then he said to him, “Rise and go; your faith has made you well.”

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “Ten Gifts, One Thank You”

*You’ll need: 10 small treats (candies, stickers, coins).*

Give one treat to each of 10 kids (or 10 volunteers). Notice how they respond — does everyone say thank you?

Then say: “Jesus healed TEN men. All ten got the gift. But only ONE came back to say thank you.”

Ask: “How do you think Jesus felt when only one came back?” Let them answer.

Say: “The one who came back got even more than just healing — Jesus said ‘your faith has made you well.’ Being thankful opens the door to something deeper.”

### Discussion Questions

- What was leprosy? Why did the ten men stand far away?

- What did they call Jesus? (Master.)

- What did Jesus tell them to do? What happened as they went?

- How many came back to say thank you? Where was he from? (A Samaritan — someone the Jews looked down on.)

- How did Jesus feel about the other nine?

- Are there gifts from God we’ve forgotten to thank Him for?

- Who could you say thank you to this week?

### Activity (8 min) — “Thankful Chain”

Give every child several paper strips (about 8–10).

On each strip they write one thing they’re thankful for (family, home, food, friends, health, church, snacks, a pet, sunshine, etc.).

Link the strips together with glue or staples into paper chains.

Connect all the chains into one long class chain and hang it up.

Say: “Look how long our chain is! God gives us SO many gifts. Let’s not be like the nine who forgot.”

### Craft (5 min) — “Thank You, Jesus” Postcard

Give each child a cardstock postcard.

On one side, they decorate a picture of themselves running back to Jesus.

On the other, they write a short thank-you note to Jesus.

Take it home and read it as a family prayer this week.

### Game (5 min) — “Thank You Tag”

One child is “it.” When they tag you, you’re frozen.

To unfreeze, another player has to come up and name something they’re thankful for.

Keep playing until everyone is frozen or the timer runs out.

Reinforces: thankful hearts free us up to move.

### Takeaways

- God gives us many blessings every single day.

- Saying thank you honors God and makes our own hearts happier.

- Don’t be one of the nine — be the one who comes back.

- A thankful heart is a heart that’s already been touched by God.
