# Jesus is the Proven Son of God — Jesus Died and Rose to Life #

**Memory Verse:** “‘Don’t be alarmed,’ he said. ‘You are looking for Jesus the Nazarene, who was crucified. He has risen! He is not here.’” — Mark 16:6 (NIV)

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “The Empty Tomb”

*You’ll need: a plastic Easter egg or small box, a rock, a small figure or picture of Jesus, and a strip of white cloth (“grave clothes”).*

Start with Jesus-figure wrapped in the cloth inside the closed egg/box. Place the rock in front of it to “seal” it.

Say: “On Friday, Jesus died on a cross for our sins. His friends were so sad. They wrapped His body and placed Him in a tomb, and rolled a huge rock in front of it. It looked like the story was over.” Tap the rock.

Say: “But on Sunday morning, something impossible happened.” Roll the rock away and open the box—show the empty cloth, no Jesus-figure. (Have it hidden behind your back.)

Then bring out the Jesus-figure standing tall and say: “HE IS RISEN! The tomb is empty because Jesus is ALIVE. Dying didn’t stop Him. That’s how we know for SURE that Jesus is God’s Son—because He beat death itself.”

### Discussion Questions

- Why did Jesus have to die? (For our sins.)

- What did the Roman soldier say when he saw how Jesus died? Why is that important?

- What did the women find when they came to the tomb early Sunday morning?

- What did the angel tell them to do?

- How does the empty tomb prove that Jesus really IS the Son of God?

- What would the story be missing if Jesus had died but NOT risen?

- If Jesus is alive today, how should that change the way we live?

### Activity (8 min) — “Friday, Saturday, Sunday” Picture Walk

Set up three stations labeled Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.

Friday: pictures of the cross. Discuss: “Jesus died for us.”

Saturday: a dark cloth. Discuss: “The disciples were sad and scared. It looked like hope was gone.”

Sunday: an empty tomb picture and sunshine. Discuss: “Jesus is ALIVE! Everything changed.”

At each station, kids add a quick drawing or word to a shared poster that shows the whole story.

### Craft (5 min) — Empty Tomb Diorama

Give each child a small paper plate folded in half to make a “cave.”

Inside, write “He is not here; He is risen!” (Mark 16:6).

On the outside, draw a large rock that has been rolled AWAY from the opening.

Glue cotton balls or tissue as the “grave clothes” left behind, empty inside.

### Game (5 min) — “Roll the Stone” Relay

Divide into teams. Give each team a large ball to represent the stone.

Each player rolls the “stone” across the room, around a cone labeled “Tomb,” and back.

At the finish line, they yell the memory phrase: “He has risen!” and tag the next player.

First team done wins. (Silly + memorable reinforcement of the resurrection.)

### Takeaways

- Jesus really died on the cross—He paid the full price for our sins.

- Jesus really rose from the dead—proving once and for all that He is the Son of God.

- Because Jesus is alive, death is not the end for anyone who trusts Him.
