# Mary and Martha — “Spending Time with Jesus” #

**Memory Verse:** “‘Mary has chosen what is better, and it will not be taken away from her.’” — Luke 10:42 (NIV)

### Scripture — Luke 10:38–42 (NIV)

³⁸ As Jesus and his disciples were on their way, he came to a village where a woman named Martha opened her home to him. ³⁹ She had a sister called Mary, who sat at the Lord’s feet listening to what he said.

⁴⁰ But Martha was distracted by all the preparations that had to be made. She came to him and asked, “Lord, don’t you care that my sister has left me to do the work by myself? Tell her to help me!”

⁴¹ “Martha, Martha,” the Lord answered, “you are worried and upset about many things, ⁴² but few things are needed—or indeed only one. Mary has chosen what is better, and it will not be taken away from her.”

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “Two Sisters, Two Choices”

*You’ll need: a busy prop (a broom, dust cloth, apron, a bunch of dishes to “wash”) and a comfy prop (a pillow or blanket).*

Put on the apron and start racing around — sweeping, wiping, muttering. “This is Martha! There’s so much to do! The floor! The table! The bread! There’s no time to sit! Someone HELP!”

Then STOP. Set down the broom. Pick up the pillow, sit calmly on the floor, and look up (like at Jesus). “This is Mary. She stopped. She sat. She listened. She was WITH Jesus.”

Ask: “Which one was Jesus proud of? Not the busy one. The one who was WITH Him.”

Say: “Serving is good. But BEING WITH Jesus is the BEST. Don’t let busy get in the way of Jesus.”

### Discussion Questions

- What was Martha doing?

- What was Mary doing?

- Why was Martha upset with her sister?

- What did Jesus say to Martha?

- Are you more like Mary or Martha most days? Why?

- When could you sit at Jesus’ feet this week? (Prayer time, Bible time, church.)

- Is being busy always bad? What did Jesus mean?

### Activity (8 min) — “Be Still” Practice

Have kids sit criss-cross on the floor.

Ask them to be completely still and quiet for 60 seconds. (You time it.)

During the quiet, encourage them silently: “Just breathe. Just listen. Imagine Jesus is right here.”

After 60 seconds, ask: “What was hard about that? What did you notice?”

Say: “God says ‘Be still and know that I am God’ (Psalm 46:10). This is what Mary was doing.”

### Craft (5 min) — “At Jesus’ Feet” Drawing

Give each child a piece of paper.

Have them draw themselves sitting at Jesus’ feet (Jesus can be a simple silhouette).

Write at the bottom: “Mary has chosen what is better.” — Luke 10:42.

Bonus: on the back, list one thing they can DO to make time to be with Jesus this week.

### Game (5 min) — “Freeze at Jesus’ Feet”

Play music. Kids dance and move around — acting “busy” like Martha.

When the music stops, everyone drops to a criss-cross “sitting-listening” pose.

Anyone still moving is out for the next round.

Reinforces: even in the middle of busy, we can STOP and be with Jesus.

### Takeaways

- Being WITH Jesus is the best choice.

- Serving is good, but time at Jesus’ feet comes first.

- Don’t let busyness rob you of Jesus.
