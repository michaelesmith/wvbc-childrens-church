# Jesus Teaches Us to Pray — “Talking to God” #

**Memory Verse:** “This, then, is how you should pray: ‘Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name…’” — Matthew 6:9 (NIV)

### Scripture — Matthew 6:5–13 (NIV)

⁵ “And when you pray, do not be like the <u>hypocrites</u>, for they love to pray standing in the synagogues and on the street corners to be seen by others. Truly I tell you, they have their reward in full.

⁶ But when you pray, go into your room, close the door and pray to your Father, who is unseen. Then your Father, who sees what is done in secret, will reward you.

⁷ And when you pray, do not keep on <u>babbling</u> like <u>pagans</u>, for they think they will be heard because of their many words. ⁸ Do not be like them, for your Father knows what you need before you ask him.

⁹ “This, then, is how you should pray:

‘Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name, ¹⁰ your kingdom come, your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven. ¹¹ Give us today our daily bread. ¹² And forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors. ¹³ And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one.’

<u>hypocrites</u>: someone who says one thing but does another. They tell others how to act, but don't act that way themselves.

<u>babbling:</u> repeating the same words over and over without really thinking about them or meaning them.

<u>pagans:</u> people who didn't know God or worship other gods

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “Direct Line to God”

*Prayer - is - talking - to - God*

### Discussion Questions

- What is prayer? (Talking with God.)

- How did some people pray in the WRONG way? (For show, with lots of empty words.)

- Where does Jesus tell us to pray? (In private, from the heart.)

- Does God already know what we need before we ask? Then why pray?

- Say the Lord’s Prayer together. What do each of the parts mean?

- What are some things YOU want to talk to God about this week?

### Activity (8 min) — Praying Through the Lord’s Prayer

Take one line of the Lord’s Prayer at a time. Discuss what it means in kid-language.

“Our Father in heaven” — God is our Dad who loves us.

“Hallowed be your name” — God’s name is holy and special.

“Your kingdom come” — Let God be in charge.

“Daily bread” — Ask God for what you need today.

“Forgive us… as we forgive” — Ask for forgiveness, and forgive others.

“Lead us not into temptation” — Help us do the right thing.

Pray it together at the end, phrase by phrase.

### Craft (5 min) — Prayer Hand

Trace each child’s hand.

Label each finger with a prayer topic: Thumb = Praise God, Index = Say Thank You, Middle = Pray for Leaders, Ring = Pray for Family, Pinky = Pray for Me.

Hang up at home as a reminder to pray every day.

### Game (5 min) — “Popcorn Prayer”

Kids sit in a circle. The leader starts by saying, “God, thank You for…” and names one thing.

The next person “pops” up and adds their own one-line prayer.

Go around the circle. No wrong answers — anything real counts.

Great for teaching kids that prayer isn’t performance; it’s conversation.

### Takeaways

- Prayer is talking to God, our Father, who loves us.

- We don’t need fancy words — God cares about our hearts.

- The Lord’s Prayer gives us a great model we can pray anytime.

- God is always listening. Anytime. Anywhere.
