# God So Loved the World — “Jesus Is God’s Gift of Love” #

**Memory Verse:** “For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.” — John 3:16 (NIV)

### Theme Specific

#### Advent Wreath — Love and the Christ Candle

Relight **Hope**, **Peace**, and **Joy**, reviewing each one. One child lights the last purple candle (**Love**): “God loved the world so much that He gave us Jesus.”

Since many kids won’t be here on Christmas Eve, light the white **Christ candle** in the center today. Have every child put a hand on the table (or hold an LED tea light) and count down together, “3, 2, 1 — Jesus is here!” as the center candle is lit.

Explain: “The white candle in the middle is for Jesus. All the waiting, hoping, and getting ready was for Him.” Say all three memory verses from Advent together (Isaiah 9:6, Luke 2:10–11, John 3:16).

#### Suggestions (to decide later)

- **Promise ornaments:** Add one ornament to a small tabletop tree each week. This week: a **heart** for God’s love (John 3:16). Look back at all three ornaments together.
- **Journey to Bethlehem:** Use a nativity set where the figures move closer to the manger each week. This week: Mary and Joseph reach the stable, the shepherds arrive, and baby Jesus is placed in the manger. The journey is complete.

### Scripture — Luke 2:1–7; John 3:16 (NIV)

⁴ So Joseph also went up from the town of Nazareth in Galilee to Judea, to Bethlehem the town of David, because he belonged to the house and line of David. ⁵ He went there to register with Mary, who was pledged to be married to him and was expecting a child. ⁶ While they were there, the time came for the baby to be born, ⁷ and she gave birth to her firstborn, a son. She wrapped him in cloths and placed him in a manger, because there was no guest room available for them.

#### John 3:16

¹⁶ For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “What Would You Give?”

*You’ll need: something you really treasure (a favorite mug, a special photo, or a prized toy from home), and a heart cut from red paper with “John 3:16” written on it.*

Hold up your treasure and tell the kids why it’s special to you. Ask: “Would I give this away? What if someone asked for it who had been really mean to me?” Let them react.

Say: “It’s hard to give away something we love. But God gave His most precious treasure — His one and only Son — to the whole world. He gave Him to people who had disobeyed Him and done wrong. That’s us!”

Hold up the heart. Say: “Why would God do that? Because He LOVES us. Christmas isn’t only about a baby in a manger. It’s about WHY He came: God so loved the world.”

End: “Jesus is the best gift ever given. And all we have to do is believe and receive Him.”

### Discussion Questions

- Why did Mary and Joseph travel to Bethlehem? (v. 1–4)

- Where was Jesus born? Why was He placed in a manger? (v. 7)

- Jesus is God’s Son, the King of everything. Why do you think He was born in such a humble place?

- John 3:16 says God “so loved the world.” Who is included in “the world”?

- What did God give because He loves us?

- What does it mean to “believe in him”?

- How can we show God’s love to others this Christmas?

### Activity (8 min) — Build the Nativity

Hand each child one nativity piece or picture card: Mary, Joseph, a donkey, the innkeeper, shepherds, sheep, an angel, a star, and the manger. Keep baby Jesus with a leader.

Read Luke 2:1–7 slowly (and recap the shepherds from last week). When a child hears their piece mentioned, they bring it up and place it in the scene.

When everything is in place, a leader adds baby Jesus last and reads John 3:16 out loud. Say: “Everyone in this scene is here because of Him — and He is here because God loves us.”

### Craft (5 min) — “For God So Loved ___” Heart Ornament

Give each child a red paper heart with a hole punched at the top and a length of ribbon.

On the front, they write: “For God so loved ______” and fill in the blank with their own name. On the back, they draw a manger or a star.

Tie on the ribbon so they can hang it on their Christmas tree at home.

### Game (5 min) — John 3:16 Scramble

Write John 3:16 on cards, a few words per card. Make one set for each team.

Mix up the cards. Teams race to put the verse in order. The first team to finish reads it aloud together.

Play again, taking away one card each round so the kids have to fill in the missing words from memory.

### Takeaways

- Jesus was born because God loves us — the whole world, and each of us by name.

- God gave His one and only Son, the greatest gift ever given.

- Whoever believes in Jesus will have eternal life.

- Christmas is about the gift of Jesus, given in love.

### Put It in Practice

Hang your heart ornament on your tree. On Christmas, read Luke 2 with your family before opening presents, and tell them John 3:16 by memory.
