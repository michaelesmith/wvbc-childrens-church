# Jesus Walks on Water — “Trusting Jesus” #

**Memory Verse:** “Then those who were in the boat worshiped him, saying, ‘Truly you are the Son of God.’” — Matthew 14:33 (NIV)

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “Eyes on Jesus”

*You’ll need: a bowl of water, a small rock, and a small floating toy (a boat or a rubber duck).*

Drop the rock into the water. Say: “This is what happens when we take our eyes off Jesus and only look at the storm. We sink.”

Float the toy on the water. Say: “This is when we keep our eyes on Jesus. Even when the wind is blowing and waves are crashing, we can stand steady.”

Say: “Peter did BOTH in this story. First he walked on water — while looking at Jesus. Then he looked at the wind, and started to sink. What did Jesus do? He didn’t let him drown. He REACHED OUT.”

End: “When you start to sink, don’t panic. Cry out: ‘Lord, save me!’ Jesus’ hand is right there.”

### Discussion Questions

- Where were the disciples? Where was Jesus?

- Why were they afraid when they saw Jesus?

- What did Peter ask Jesus?

- What happened when Peter looked at the wind instead of Jesus?

- What did Peter cry out?

- What did Jesus do?

- What are the “storms” in your life right now? What does it look like to keep your eyes on Jesus?

### Activity (8 min) — “Water Walk”

Lay blue construction-paper “stepping stones” across the floor in a curved path.

At one end, place a picture of Jesus. At the other end, kids start in the “boat” (a taped-off square).

One by one, kids walk the stones toward Jesus. Others call “wind!” or “waves!” to distract them.

If they keep their eyes on the Jesus picture, they make it. If they look away, they “sink” (step off).

Not really about winning — about experiencing what it feels like to focus on Jesus.

### Craft (5 min) — “Reach Out” Handprint

Trace one small hand (Peter’s reaching hand) and one larger hand (Jesus’ saving hand) on paper.

Overlap them so they look like they’re grabbing each other.

Write around them: “Lord, save me.” — Matthew 14:30

Reminder: Jesus’ hand is always there when we reach out.

### Game (5 min) — “Red Light, Green Light” (Storm Style)

One kid is “Jesus” at the front. Everyone else lines up at the back.

“Jesus” calls: “Eyes on Me!” — kids walk forward. “Look at the storm!” — kids must freeze.

Anyone who moves during “storm” has to go back a step (“sink”).

First one to reach “Jesus” wins and becomes the caller for the next round.

### Takeaways

- Storms scare us — but Jesus is always near, even in the middle of them.

- Keep your eyes on Jesus, not the storm.

- When we start to sink, Jesus’ hand is right there. Just cry out.

- Storms end. Jesus stays.
