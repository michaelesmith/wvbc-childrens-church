# The Cheerful Giver — “Thankful Hearts Share” #

**Memory Verse:** “Thanks be to God for his indescribable gift!” — 2 Corinthians 9:15 (NIV)

### Theme Specific

#### Thankful Leaves

Finish the Thankful Wall. Each child adds one last leaf, then place a large center leaf that says **“Jesus.”** Look back at the whole wall together and read some of the leaves from the past four weeks.

### Scripture — 2 Corinthians 9:6–15 (NIV)

⁶ Remember this: Whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows generously will also reap generously. ⁷ Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver. ⁸ And God is able to bless you abundantly, so that in all things at all times, having all that you need, you will abound in every good work. ⁹ As it is written:

“They have freely scattered their gifts to the poor; \
their righteousness endures forever.”

¹⁰ Now he who supplies seed to the sower and bread for food will also supply and increase your store of seed and will enlarge the harvest of your righteousness. ¹¹ You will be enriched in every way so that you can be generous on every occasion, and through us your generosity will result in thanksgiving to God.

¹² This service that you perform is not only supplying the needs of the Lord’s people but is also overflowing in many expressions of thanks to God. ¹³ Because of the service by which you have proved yourselves, others will praise God for the obedience that accompanies your confession of the gospel of Christ, and for your generosity in sharing with them and with everyone else. ¹⁴ And in their prayers for you their hearts will go out to you, because of the surpassing grace God has given you. ¹⁵ Thanks be to God for his indescribable gift!

### Object Lesson (5 min) — “A Few Seeds or a Lot?”

*You’ll need: a packet of seeds (or dried beans), two small pots or cups of soil, and a picture of a big harvest (a full garden or a field of corn).*

Drop just ONE seed into the first pot. Ask: “If I plant one seed, how much food will I get?” (Not much!)

Pour a big handful of seeds into the second pot. Ask: “What if I plant a LOT of seeds?” Show the harvest picture.

Say: “Paul told the church that giving is like planting. ‘Whoever sows sparingly will also reap sparingly, and whoever sows generously will also reap generously.’ When we give, God grows something good — and the people we help thank God too!”

End: “And remember — every seed we have came from God in the first place. The more thankful we are, the more we want to share.”

### Discussion Questions

- What did Paul ask the church in Corinth to do? (Collect a gift for poor believers in Jerusalem.)

- What does a “cheerful giver” look like? What does a grumpy giver look like?

- Where does everything we have come from? (v. 8–11 — God.)

- When we share, who else ends up thanking God? (v. 12–13)

- Besides money, what can kids give? (Time, toys, help, kind words.)

- Paul thanks God for His “indescribable gift.” What is that gift?

- Why is Jesus the greatest gift God has ever given?

### Activity (8 min) — Thanksgiving Blessing Bags

As a class, fill small bags for a church shut-in, a local food pantry, or the church staff.

Set out items in an assembly line (snacks, hand warmers, candy, tissues, bookmarks). Each child adds something to each bag — cheerfully!

Talk while you work: “Who will open this bag? How do you think they’ll feel? Who will they thank?”

Pray over the bags together before they go out.

### Craft (5 min) — “I Thank God for You” Cards

Give each child a folded card and markers.

On the front, kids write “I Thank God for You!” and draw a leaf, turkey, or gift.

Inside, they write a short note or draw a picture for the person who will receive the blessing bag.

Tuck one card into each bag.

### Game (5 min) — Pass the Blessing

Kids sit in a circle and pass a wrapped box while music plays.

When the music stops, the child holding the box names someone they could share with this week and how (a toy, help, a kind word).

At the end, open the box together. Inside is a note: “Jesus — God’s greatest gift.”

Say: “Thanksgiving leads right into Christmas — when we celebrate God giving us Jesus!”

### Takeaways

- A thankful heart shares — God loves a cheerful giver.

- Everything we have comes from God, so we can share it.

- When we give, the people we help thank God too, so thankfulness spreads.

- Jesus is God’s indescribable gift — the best gift of all.

### Put It in Practice

At Thanksgiving dinner, have each person share one thing they’re thankful for and one way they’ll share with someone this season.
